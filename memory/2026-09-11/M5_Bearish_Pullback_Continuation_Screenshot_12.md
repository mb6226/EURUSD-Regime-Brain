## Pattern

### Bearish Pullback Continuation

The chart shows a strong bearish impulse followed by a controlled pullback. The pullback fails to recover the prior structure, forms a lower high, and then price resumes the bearish move.

### Visual sequence

```text
Strong bearish impulse
        ↓
   Do NOT chase
        ↓
     Pullback
        ↑
Re-test broken structure / resistance
        ↓
     Rejection
        ↓
    Lower High
        ↓
Bearish momentum returns
        ↓
   SELL confirmation
        ↓
Continuation toward prior low / liquidity
```

---

## What the Arrow Highlights

The yellow arrow identifies the **pullback/rejection area** after the initial bearish displacement.

This is the important execution zone.

The lesson is **not** to sell the first large bearish candle or chase price after the move has already extended.

Instead:

1. Wait for the bearish impulse.
2. Wait for price to pull back.
3. Watch whether the pullback reaches broken structure, resistance, or dynamic resistance.
4. Look for rejection.
5. Confirm that the pullback has produced a **Lower High**.
6. Enter SELL only when bearish momentum resumes.

---

## Real-Time Detection Rule

The Brain must try to recognize this pattern **while it is forming**, not after the move is complete.

### Live checklist

- [ ] Strong bearish displacement occurred.
- [ ] Price has extended away from the origin.
- [ ] No chasing at the end of the impulse.
- [ ] Pullback is forming.
- [ ] Pullback reaches a meaningful resistance / broken structure.
- [ ] Rejection appears.
- [ ] Pullback fails to make a new meaningful high.
- [ ] Lower High is confirmed.
- [ ] Bearish momentum resumes.
- [ ] SELL has acceptable R:R.
- [ ] SL can be placed above the pullback high.
- [ ] Target is previous low / next liquidity.

---

## Execution Model

### Preferred entry

**SELL on confirmation after the pullback rejection / Lower High.**

### Stop Loss

Above the structural pullback high.

### Targets

1. Previous swing low
2. Next downside liquidity
3. Extended target only if momentum remains strong

### Core principle

> **The pullback is the opportunity, not the problem.**

A strong impulse creates distance. The pullback gives the Brain a chance to enter with a tighter structural stop and better risk/reward.
